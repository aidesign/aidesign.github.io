Demin’s Kitchen Atelier brings premium Austrian kitchen furniture to New York — an extensive library of materials, finishes, and styles for every taste, from modern to classic.

Turnkey Process

Leave a request, and we'll walk you through every stage of building your kitchen with us

1

Consultation

Initial meeting in our Brooklyn showroom or virtually. We discuss your routines, lifestyle, budget, and spatial parameters.

2

Design & 3D Render

Photorealistic 3D visualization, tactile sample moodboards, architectural electrical layout, and cabinet zoning.

3

Production

Automated high-precision manufacturing in Austria followed by custom benchwork assembly and strict quality inspection.

4

Installation

Clean, certified installation in 2–3 days by our in-house master carpenters. Seamless alignment and zero construction mess.
